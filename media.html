Press & Recognition

Le Pur Organics in the Media

Independent coverage of Le Pur Organics, collected in one place. In 2023, Dawn, Pakistan's leading English-language newspaper, featured the brand in its Going Loco for Local series, which champions independent Pakistani businesses. The review called the Nemoringa Cleansing Balm a game changer and placed Le Pur Organics among the local brands changing the country's skincare landscape.

Official brand information site. Coverage is written and published independently by the outlet linked below.

Dawn Images · 4 August 2023

Going loco for local: Le Pur Organics’ Nemoringa Cleansing Balm is a game changer

By Marsha Tayyab · Rated 4 out of 5 by Dawn Images

“The makeup removing balm has taken my double cleansing routine up a notch and I have nothing but praise for it.”Dawn Images, August 2023

The review focused on the balm's texture, its ability to melt away makeup, and its place in a double-cleansing routine. It also noted the ingredients behind the formula, including grapeseed oil, shea butter, moringa oil, moringa extract, neem oil, neem extract, beeswax and tea tree essential oil.

Why It Matters

Dawn's Going Loco for Local Series

Going Loco for Local is Dawn Images' ongoing series on independent Pakistani businesses. Each product is evaluated by a member of the Images team, and the series has become a reference point for shoppers looking for local alternatives in beauty, fashion and design.

Being reviewed in that context matters. It is not a paid placement or a brand-written profile. It is an independent editorial judgement published by one of Pakistan's most established media organisations.

The Bigger Picture

Recognition for a Local Category

When Le Pur Organics launched in 2017, certified organic skincare was a small category in Pakistan. Coverage in a national title helped move the conversation from “is local skincare good enough?” to “which local skincare is worth buying?”

That shift is bigger than one product. It is recognition for the formulators, small-batch makers and independent brands building a credible clean-beauty sector in Pakistan.

The Product

Nemoringa Cleansing Balm

The product at the centre of Dawn's review is a waterless botanical cleansing balm. It is used as a first cleanse to melt makeup, mineral sunscreen and daily grime, and as a comforting salve for dry or irritated patches.

The current product page describes a blend of grapeseed, coconut and castor oils, shea butter, beeswax, sunflower oil, moringa seed oil and leaf powder, neem oil and leaf powder, tea tree essential oil, spirulina and vitamin E.
