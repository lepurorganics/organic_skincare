The Le Pur Organics Signature Collection: Our Top 10 Products

Ten formulas do most of the work in the Le Pur Organics range. This guide explains what each one is built around, who it suits and how to use it, then links straight to the official product page so you can check the current size, price and stock.

How to Use This Guide

Skincare is easier when you stop shopping by product type and start shopping by concern. Each entry below follows the same structure: the key actives, the skin it is best for, and the simplest way to use it.

If you are building a routine from scratch, read the first two products, then jump to the comparison table and the morning and evening routine near the end. If you already know your concern, use the table to shortlist two or three formulas.

No prices are listed here because they change. The official product page always has the current information.

Signature

1. Age Defying Face Oil

The product the brand calls its signature. A botanical facial oil for skin that looks tired, thin or dry, with a texture light enough to wear in humid weather.

Key Actives

  • Bakuchiol, a plant-derived retinol alternative
  • Coenzyme Q10 and vitamin E for antioxidant support
  • Plant-derived squalane, jojoba and argan oils
  • Sea buckthorn and rosehip fruit oils
  • Apple and argan stem cell extract

Best For

Fine lines and loss of bounce, dullness, uneven-looking tone, dryness, and combination skin that wants nourishment without a heavy finish.

How to Use

Warm two to three drops between your palms and press into damp skin on the face and neck, morning and night. Follow with sunscreen in the morning.

Top Seller

2. Age Miracle Serum

A hydration-first serum for skin that feels tight, looks dull or is starting to show fine lines. It layers easily under a moisturiser or facial oil.

Formula Focus

  • Signal peptides
  • Essential skin vitamins
  • Probiotic ferment technology
  • Hyaluronic acid for multi-layer hydration

Best For

Dehydration, fine lines, loss of firmness and dullness. The brand describes the finish as dewy and non-greasy, with no added fragrance.

How to Use

Apply to clean skin before moisturiser or facial oil, morning and evening. At night it works well layered under Age Defying Face Oil.

View Age Miracle Serum on lepurorganics.com
Brightening

3. Sérum De C

A vitamin C serum built for dullness, dark spots and an uneven-looking tone. It combines antioxidant care with hydration, which makes it easier to tolerate than many brightening serums.

Key Actives

  • Vitamin C
  • Ferulic acid
  • Hyaluronic acid
  • Panthenol and glycerin

Best For

Dullness, uneven tone, sun-related discolouration and post-acne marks. Also a sensible first brightening step for anyone new to vitamin C.

How to Use

Apply in the morning to clean skin before moisturiser. Always follow with broad-spectrum sunscreen, because brightening actives work best alongside daily sun protection.

View Sérum De C on lepurorganics.com
Pigmentation

4. Créme De Pigmentation

A nightly tone-correcting cream for dark spots, post-acne marks and uneven-looking patches. The formula pairs brightening actives with enough moisturising care to keep skin comfortable.

Key Actives

  • Kojic acid dipalmitate
  • Niacinamide (vitamin B3)
  • Squalane
  • Panthenol (provitamin B5)
  • Sea buckthorn and calendula extracts

Best For

Dark spots, post-acne marks, dullness and an uneven-looking complexion, especially where brightening products have felt too drying.

How to Use

Apply a thin layer to areas of concern at night, after serum. Use sunscreen daily while using brightening actives, and avoid stacking several strong brightening products in the same routine.

View Créme De Pigmentation on lepurorganics.com
Family Care

5. Jar of Gentleness

A face and body cream for dry, sensitive or reactive skin. The brand positions it as a universal moisturiser gentle enough for the whole family, including children.

Key Actives

  • Sweet almond oil
  • Shea butter
  • Humectants for multi-depth hydration
  • A subtle lavender scent

Best For

Dryness, flaking, tightness, ashiness and sensitive skin. Useful anywhere you need one moisturiser for both face and body.

How to Use

Massage into clean skin as needed, morning or night. It can be used on the face, body and dry patches, and layered over a serum for extra barrier support.

View Jar of Gentleness on lepurorganics.com
Cleansing

6. Multitasker

An oil-to-milk cleanser that removes makeup and sunscreen without leaving skin tight. The brand also lists it for body massage, baby massage and hair conditioning.

Formula Focus

  • A blend of plant oils
  • Plant-derived emulsifiers that turn the oil milky with water
  • Rinses without a greasy residue

Best For

Makeup removal, waterproof sunscreen, a first cleanse in a double-cleanse routine, and anyone who finds foaming cleansers stripping.

How to Use

Massage onto dry skin, add warm water to emulsify into a milk, then rinse. Follow with a water-based cleanser if you want a full double cleanse.

View Multitasker on lepurorganics.com
Oily & Acne-Prone

7. Black Beauty

A cold-process charcoal bar for oily, congested and breakout-prone skin. It is designed to clean deeply while keeping the lather creamy rather than stripping.

Key Actives

  • Activated charcoal
  • Neem leaf water and neem powder
  • Turmeric root powder
  • Tea tree essential oil
  • Saponified coconut, palm, olive, sunflower and castor oils

Best For

Oily skin, clogged pores, body acne and anyone who prefers a matte finish after cleansing.

How to Use

Lather between wet hands, massage over damp skin and rinse. Use once or twice daily depending on how your skin tolerates it.

View Black Beauty on lepurorganics.com
Spot Correction

8. Be Daagh

A targeted cream for stubborn dark spots and post-acne marks. It is the more focused option in the range, designed to sit on the area that needs it rather than all over the face.

Key Actives

  • Alpha-arbutin
  • Kojic acid dipalmitate
  • Niacinamide (vitamin B3)
  • Panthenol and glycerin
  • Grapeseed and sunflower oils

Best For

Stubborn dark spots, sun damage, age spots and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation left by breakouts.

How to Use

Apply a small amount directly to spots or uneven areas, once or twice daily. Follow with sunscreen in the morning and introduce it gradually if your skin is sensitive.

View Be Daagh on lepurorganics.com
Eye Care

9. Eye Cream

A lightweight eye cream for the thin, high-demand skin around the eyes. It is built around de-puffing, brightening the look of shadowed contours and smoothing dehydration lines.

Formula Focus

  • A multi-action eye elixir with a weightless finish
  • Supports micro-circulation for morning puffiness
  • Targets the look of dark circles and uneven tone
  • Sustained hydration for crepey texture

Best For

Morning puffiness, dark circles, fine dehydration lines and anyone who wants a light eye product that layers well under makeup.

How to Use

Pat a small amount around the orbital bone morning and evening, using your ring finger. Avoid dragging the skin and keep the product out of the eyes.

View Eye Cream on lepurorganics.com
Hair Care

10. Scalp Serum

A rosemary and argan scalp treatment for hair that sheds more than it should and a scalp that feels dry or sluggish. It is applied to the roots, not the lengths.

Key Actives

  • Rosemary essential oil
  • Rosemary-infused argan and olive oils
  • Pure argan oil
  • Rosemary leaf extract

Best For

Hair fall, weak roots, a dry or flaky scalp and anyone trying to retain length by reducing breakage at the root.

How to Use

Part the hair and apply to the scalp, then massage gently. Leave it on before washing, then shampoo as usual. Use two to three times a week.

Build a Routine

A Simple Morning and Evening Routine

This is one example, not a prescription. Introduce one new product at a time, patch test first, and adjust for your skin. If you use prescription actives, ask your dermatologist before layering.

Morning

  1. Cleanse with Black Beauty for oily skin, or rinse and use Multitasker if you need to remove overnight product.
  2. Apply Sérum De C for brightness.
  3. Add Age Miracle Serum if your skin feels dehydrated.
  4. Pat Eye Cream around the orbital bone.
  5. Use Jar of Gentleness on dry areas, then finish with broad-spectrum sunscreen.

Evening

  1. Massage Multitasker onto dry skin, emulsify with water and rinse. Follow with a water-based cleanser if needed.
  2. Apply Be Daagh or Créme De Pigmentation to targeted areas, not both at once.
  3. Layer Age Miracle Serum for hydration.
  4. Seal with Age Defying Face Oil.
  5. Use Scalp Serum on the scalp two to three times a week before washing.

Questions

Product Questions

Which Le Pur Organics product is best for beginners?

Start with a gentle cleanser, one serum for your main concern, a moisturiser and sunscreen. Jar of Gentleness works as a simple moisturiser, while Sérum De C or Age Miracle Serum covers brightness or hydration. Add a targeted product only when you know how your skin responds.

Can I use Sérum De C with Age Defying Face Oil?

Yes. A simple approach is Sérum De C in the morning under sunscreen, and Age Defying Face Oil at night to seal in hydration. If your skin is sensitive, introduce one at a time and patch test first.

Which product is best for dark spots?

Sérum De C supports overall brightness, while Be Daagh and Créme De Pigmentation are the targeted options for dark spots and post-acne marks. Daily broad-spectrum sunscreen is essential while using any brightening product.

Are Le Pur products suitable for oily or acne-prone skin?

The range includes Black Beauty, an activated charcoal cleansing bar, and Multitasker, an oil-to-milk cleanser. Age Defying Face Oil is described by the brand as non-comedogenic and lightweight. Patch test new products and introduce them one at a time.

How long does it take to see results?

Le Pur Organics says most users notice improvements within two to four weeks of consistent use. Pigmentation and deep lines usually take longer, and results vary by skin type and routine.
